How it bends

Three taps. No take-up arithmetic in your head, no notebook, no second app.

  1. Enter the offset depth

    Type the depth you need, in inches. The keypad does arithmetic too — add and subtract before you bend.

  2. Tap the angle

    10°, 22.5°, 30°, 45°, 60° — the trade multipliers you already know, with exact 1/sin values when you want them.

  3. Read the distance

    One number, large and amber: the distance between your marks. Nothing else on screen to misread.
