Privacy Policy

FirstBend (Android app, com.firstbend.calculator) · Effective 19 September 2026

FirstBend is a conduit-bending calculator. It does not need an account, and it does not want your data. This page explains, in plain language, the little that is processed and the much that is not.

What the app processes

  • Purchase information, through RevenueCat. When you buy FirstBend Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. — a purchase-management service — to record that the unlock is yours. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app-user identifier and your purchase and entitlement status (whether Pro is active). This is what lets “Restore purchase” work on a new phone.
  • Payment, through Google Play. The purchase itself is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card details, name, or billing address.

What the app never collects

  • No name, email address, or phone number
  • No contacts, photos, files, or messages
  • No location
  • No advertising identifiers, no ads
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No account, and no sign-in

How it is used

The purchase identifier is used for one thing: unlocking Pro and restoring it. Nothing is sold, rented, or shared for advertising, and nothing is used to build a profile of you.

Who else is involved

Two service providers, each doing one job: RevenueCat (purchase management, acting on our behalf) and Google Play (payment processing). Their own privacy policies apply to how they handle data in their role.

Retention and deletion

The anonymous purchase record is kept while you own Pro, so the unlock keeps working. Because there is no account, we cannot identify you by name — but if you want the purchase record associated with your installation deleted, email us and we will do it (note: deleting it also removes your Pro unlock, unless the store purchase is still active).

Security

All communication with RevenueCat and Google Play happens over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Children

FirstBend is a trade tool intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from them.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Google Play, so refunds follow Google Play’s policy — request them through the Play Store’s order history.

This website

This site is static: it sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads nothing from third-party servers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may keep standard request logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operations, under GitHub’s own privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s store listing.

Contact
